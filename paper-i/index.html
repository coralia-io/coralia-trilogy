The Coralia Sequence: A Unique Finite Integer Set Under Fibonacci-Lucas Terminal Constraints

Emma Cecile · 2026-01-02 · Version 1.1 · Preprint

Abstract

We introduce the Coralia Sequence C = {0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12, 15, 23, 30, 35}, a 12-element integer set characterized by nine axioms (C1–C9). The sequence is generated by four arithmetic zones with ascending gaps followed by a descending Fibonacci–Lucas cascade. We prove that C is the unique 12-element completion of a 10-element seed determined by the axioms. The terminal gap triple (8, 7, 5) = (F₆, L₄, F₅) is shown to be the unique strictly descending triple from Fib ∪ Luc summing to 20 with all elements at least 5.
