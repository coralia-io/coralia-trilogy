Axioms That Predict What They Don't Mention: Empirical Content of the Coralia Constraints

Emma Cecile · 2026-01-05 · Version 1.0 · Preprint

Abstract

A previous paper proved that the Coralia Sequence C = {0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12, 15, 23, 30, 35} is the unique 12-element subset of {0, …, 35} satisfying axioms C1–C9. This paper asks: why do these axioms work? Under a nearest-integer classifier, e/π-analytic expressions land on C at rate 5/6 while recurrence-defined expressions land at rate 1/13—a separation of 75.6 percentage points (Cohen’s h = 1.74 [2], Fisher p = 0.003 [3]). The axioms predict where continuous limits stabilize when discretized. A subsequent paper addresses the mechanism.
